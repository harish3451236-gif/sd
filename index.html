Questions people ask

Cannot find your answer? Call or WhatsApp us.

How many guests can the hall hold?
The main hall seats 500 guests. The dining hall seats 400 at once.
Can I bring my own caterer?
Yes. We also have a vegetarian kitchen if you want to cook here.
How much parking is there?
Free parking for 40 cars and 40 bikes.
What if the power goes off?
We have full generator backup, so the muhurtham is never stopped.
How do I confirm my date?
Contact us to confirm your date and discuss booking details.
Can I see the hall before booking?
Yes. Call or WhatsApp us and we will fix a time for you.
What if I need to cancel?
Please contact us on WhatsApp to discuss cancellations.
